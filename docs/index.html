Everything you want to check between levels

The app pulls your data straight from the RetroAchievements Web API and adapts the layout to your screen, from the small, nearly square Pocket Classic to 16:9 widescreens.

Playing

Your 50 most recently played games, with progress bars and when you last played each one.

Library

Every game you've unlocked something in. Sort by recent activity, progress or name, and see your mastered and beaten games tagged.

Want to Play

Your RetroAchievements Want to Play list, with achievement counts and points. Add games to it from any game's screen.

For You

Suggestions picked for you: games you're close to mastering, games people you follow are playing that you haven't started, and random picks from your Want to Play list.

Following

Everyone you follow, with their points. Open a player to see what they've played and unlocked lately, and their progress in any game.

Search and browse

Search every game with achievements by name, or pick a console and browse its full list.

Unlocks

Everything you've unlocked in the last 30 days. Pick one to jump to its game.

Everything about a game

Screenshots, box art, developer, release date and your playtime, plus every achievement with its unlock rate, RetroPoints and missable tags. Green is hardcore, silver is softcore.

Fits your screen and style

One column on the Pocket Classic, a grid on 4:3 and 16:9. Pick an accent color, like teal, and choose which tabs to show.

No touchscreen needed

Every screen works with the built-in buttons. A green outline shows what's selected, and going back returns you to the row you were on. Touch works too.

Install in a minute

Wasabi isn't on the Play Store. You install the APK directly.

  1. Download the APK

    Open this page on your handheld and tap Download APK, or copy the file over from your computer.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.

  3. Get your Web API key

    On retroachievements.org, go to Settings → Authentication → Web API Key. Use the key, not your password. It stays on your device.

  4. Sign in

    Enter your username and key, and your progress loads. From then on, Wasabi tells you when an update is out and installs it for you.
